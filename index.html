How Townaija works

No roadside extortion. No ringing 10 private numbers. Name your price, review verified offers, and pick the best operator.

01

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about pricing, coverage, and operator onboarding.

How much does a tow cost?

You specify what you wish to offer. The app calculates a transparent recommended fare based on actual route mileage and the truck classification your vehicle requires. Nearby operators accept or counter. You only pay what you confirmed.

How do I pay the operator?

You pay the operator directly via cash or immediate Nigerian bank transfer once your car is delivered at your destination. Confirm payment in the app to finalize the job.

Where is Townaija live?

We are active in Abuja and surrounding major corridors today. Lagos, Port Harcourt, and Ibadan rollouts follow shortly.

What are the requirements to drive with Townaija?

You need a certified tow vehicle (wheel-lift, flatbed, or heavy-duty), valid driver's licence, government ID, and an Android device. Register inside the app and upload clear documents for 24-hour verification.

What if my vehicle is scratched or damaged?

Tap "Report a problem" inside your completed or active job screen. Our dispute desk immediately audits the pre-tow condition photos against the delivery photos and steps in.
